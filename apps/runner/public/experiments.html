Inspect the existing constitution
The Governor reserves the FleetGov bond in the proposal transaction. Enough participation returns it even after defeat. Cancellation or insufficient participation transfers it to a non-voting treasury, reducing the proposer's future voting power. Voting costs no bond. Higher proposal thresholds test delegation and coalition building. A fresh operator-authorised experiment redistributes the original five-token supply after the old run closes. Agents cannot mint more or reset a run.
